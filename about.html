Our story

It started with one chair and a waiting list of eleven

In 2016, Aarohi Mehta opened a single-chair studio above a bookshop on Linking Road. No receptionist, no phone line — just skill, honesty, and a notebook of appointments that filled faster than she could turn pages.

A decade later, Lumière is fifty stylists across eight cities — but the notebook rule survives: never promise a slot you can't honour, and never sell a treatment a client doesn't need. This platform is that rule, written in software.
